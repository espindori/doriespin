READING 6 — SATURDAY

SAMUEL BECKETT
Part 3 from Proust

Proust had a bad memory, as he had an inefficient habit, because he had an inefficient habit. The man with a good memory does not remember anything, because he does not forget anything.

His memory is uniform, a creature of routine, at once a condition and function of his impeccable habit, an instrument of reference instead of an instrument of discovery.
